# Code of conduct

We want FiscaLLM to be a friendly, respectful place for everyone who uses or contributes to it.

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent, and ask before you assume otherwise.
- Disagree with ideas, not people. No harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Keep issues and pull requests on topic and constructive.
- Do not share other people's private information.

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Maintainers may edit or remove comments, and block people who break these rules.

To report a problem, email **theaicommit@gmail.com**. Reports are handled privately.
